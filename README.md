## `$ whoami`

I'm a Full-Stack Web Developer in Bengaluru, India, currently building at **Athena Technology Solutions**, where I'm leading the migration of our marketing site from WordPress to Next.js, chasing every millisecond of load time and every point of Lighthouse score.

Before this, I spent close to two years at **Zibtek** shipping cross-browser, accessibility-first interfaces for client products, and picking up the habit of never shipping a page without checking its Core Web Vitals.

On the side, I run **Clay Tech**, a small digital agency built around one idea: *Build. Optimize. Grow.*

Unusual footnote: I started out with a degree in **Botany**, not computer science. Turns out three years of dissecting plant systems is surprisingly good training for dissecting a slow render tree. Same instinct, different specimen.
